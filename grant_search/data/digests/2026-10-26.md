# Grant digest — 2026-10-26

HFNJ's application mechanics turn out to change what the November 19 date means
— and the next step is much smaller than writing an application.

## HFNJ is a two-step process, and step one has not been taken

**Standard applicants cannot submit a proposal directly.** They must first
complete an **Initial Request for Funding Form**. HFNJ then contacts the
organizations it wants to move forward with, and the full application link is
only released after that permission.

So **2026-11-19 is the deadline for a proposal this organization is not yet
eligible to submit.** The gate is the initial form, and nothing has gone in.

That is good news and bad news in one. The bad news: with 24 days left, a
two-step sequence that requires HFNJ to review a request and respond before the
proposal deadline is already tight. The good news: **the thing that needs doing
is short.** Not a logic model, not a budget, not 106 placeholders — an initial
request form. Everything else only matters if HFNJ says yes.

This is now the most time-sensitive item in the pipeline, and the smallest.

## What the full application will ask for, once invited

Worth knowing in advance, because it maps cleanly onto the org kit:

- **Application text, a logic model, and a budget** — the org kit has templates
  for all three, in `06-logic-model-and-evaluation.md` and `07-budget.md`
- **Supplemental:** project budget and the IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter
- **Budget form:** columns for agency resources, reimbursements and fees, other
  grants (marked requested or granted), and the HFNJ request. Only the HFNJ
  Request column is required to submit. **Income must equal expenses.**
- Most common award type is a **12-month project grant**

## Two things that change how to write it

**Indirect costs are not automatically funded** — case by case. This is the
*opposite* of the federal posture in the org kit, which says always elect the
10% de minimis rate. Do not carry that advice across to HFNJ.

**Sustainability plans play a critical role in review**, per HFNJ's own
guidelines. The org kit's sustainability section is entirely placeholdered. If
the invitation comes, that is the section to write first rather than last — and
it is the one that cannot be improvised, because it requires knowing the
organization's actual revenue mix.

## Scope confirmed

HFNJ accepts applications only in medical and behavioral health care —
explicitly including **behavioral health, workforce training, and public health
and prevention**. This organization sits squarely inside that. Food insecurity,
general transportation and research are no longer accepted, none of which
affects this application.

One date conflict noted: a third-party listing gives a May 20, 2026 deadline for
Community Health Grants, which has passed and contradicts HFNJ's own page.
Rely on hfnj.org.

## Where this leaves things

Over four weeks this routine has removed every external obstacle in front of
HFNJ: the affiliation question answered by looking at actual grantees, the
programmatic scope confirmed, the funder's activity and scale established, a
close comparator identified, and now the application mechanics mapped.

What remains is one short form, submitted by a person. If it goes in this week,
there is a realistic path to a proposal by November 19. If it does not, HFNJ
joins September's five — and the next genuine opportunities are the county
opioid settlement cycles in early 2027, which at least have runway.
